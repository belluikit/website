---
title: 3D charts
description: Bar3D, Scatter3D, Surface and Line3D series, drawn in a 3D scene the user can orbit.
weight: 5
---

The 3D series draw into a 3D scene in the plot area, with axes and a ground
grid. Drag to orbit the camera around the scene and scroll to zoom. The chart
keeps the camera where the user left it when it redraws.

Coordinates are scene units, not pixels or axis values, and the default camera
looks at the origin from a few units away. Keep your data within a few units
of the origin, or scale it there, and it will be in view. Every 3D series has a
`Camera` field that replaces the default camera with a
[`canvas.Camera3D`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/belluikit/bellui/canvas#Camera3D)
of your own.

3D series show no tooltips: the chart does not hit-test them.

## Bar3D

A [`Bar3DSeries`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/belluikit/bellui/x/charts/series#Bar3DSeries)
stands a bar on each cell of an X-Z grid. Use it for a value that depends on
two categories, such as sales by region and year.

{{< screenshot name="charts-bar3d" alt="Nine 3D bars on a three by three grid, each in a different colour" >}}

{{< example file="extensions/charts/series/bar3d/main.go" region="bar3d" >}}

Each [`Bar3DData`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/belluikit/bellui/x/charts/series#Bar3DData)
has grid positions `X` and `Z` and a `Value`. Heights are scaled so that the
tallest bar is 2 units high. `BarSize` is the width of each bar's footprint
(default 0.4). Each bar takes the next colour from the built-in palette unless
you set `Bar3DData.Color`.

## Scatter3D

A [`Scatter3DSeries`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/belluikit/bellui/x/charts/series#Scatter3DSeries)
places a small solid at each point in space.

{{< screenshot name="charts-scatter3d" alt="Sixty points rising in a spiral around the vertical axis" >}}

{{< example file="extensions/charts/series/scatter3d/main.go" region="scatter3d" >}}

`Data` stores x, y, z triplets flat: `[x0, y0, z0, x1, y1, z1, ...]`. Values
are used as scene coordinates as they are, with Y pointing up. `PointSize` sets
the size of each point (default 0.08), and `Color` overrides the palette
colour.

## Surface

A [`SurfaceSeries`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/belluikit/bellui/x/charts/series#SurfaceSeries)
draws a height field: a grid of values as a coloured surface.

{{< screenshot name="charts-surface" alt="A 3D surface with a peak in the middle and a ring-shaped trough around it, coloured from blue at the bottom to yellow at the top" >}}

{{< example file="extensions/charts/series/surface/main.go" region="surface" >}}

`Rows` and `Cols` give the grid size, and `Data` holds the heights row by row,
like a heatmap: `Data[r*Cols+c]`. The surface is scaled to fit the scene: the
grid spans 3 units across and the heights 2 units, whatever the units of your
data. `XMin`, `XMax`, `ZMin` and `ZMax` describe the grid's extent in data
units (by default 0 to `Cols-1` and 0 to `Rows-1`).
`ColorStops` colours the surface by height, from 0 at the lowest to 1 at the
highest; without stops it runs from blue through green to yellow.

## Line3D

A [`Line3DSeries`](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/belluikit/bellui/x/charts/series#Line3DSeries)
draws a line as a flat ribbon in space. It has two modes:

- **A path in space.** Leave `ZPosition` nil and give `Data` as x, y, z
  triplets.
- **A 2D line at a depth.** Set `ZPosition` and give `Data` as x, y pairs. Several
  series at different depths stand one behind the other, like the 3D line
  charts of a spreadsheet.

{{< screenshot name="charts-line3d" alt="Three 3D ribbons for revenue, profit and cost, one behind the other" >}}

{{< example file="extensions/charts/series/line3d/main.go" region="line3d" >}}

`LineWidth` sets the ribbon's width (default 0.15), and `Color` overrides the
palette colour. Each series draws its own set of axes sized to its own data, so
a chart with several 3D lines shows overlapping axes.
