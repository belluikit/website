_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

bellui.com is run by TrendVidia. This page explains what happens to
information about you when you visit the site. If you have a question, write to
[bellui@trendvidia.com](mailto:bellui@trendvidia.com).

## In short

- We do not ask you for any personal information. There are no accounts, no
  forms and no newsletter.
- We do not set cookies of our own, and we do not run analytics.
- The site shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies
  to show and measure those ads. In the European Economic Area, the United
  Kingdom and Switzerland they only do so after you agree. In the United
  States you can opt out.

## Advertising

The site uses [Google AdSense](https://adsense.google.com) to show ads. When a
page loads, your browser contacts Google's ad servers, which receive your IP
address, your browser details and the address of the page you are reading.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your
earlier visits to this site and to other websites. Google's advertising cookies
let it and its partners show you ads based on those visits.

You can turn off personalised ads from Google in
[My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/). You can also turn off
personalised ads from many other ad vendors at
[aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) or, in Europe,
[youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/). With personalised ads
off you still see ads, but they are not based on your browsing.

Google explains what it does with this information in
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and in its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Your consent in Europe

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or
Switzerland, a message from Google asks whether you agree to cookies and
personalised ads before any are used. You can accept, refuse, or choose by
purpose. If you refuse, Google may still show ads that are not personalised.
You can change your choice at any time through the privacy and cookie settings
link that the message leaves on the page.

### Your choices in the United States

Several US states, including California, give residents the right to opt out
of the "sale" or "sharing" of their personal information. Using cookies to pick
ads based on your browsing on other websites counts as sharing under those
laws.

If you visit from one of those states, a message from Google offers a link to
opt out. When you opt out, Google limits how it uses information from your
visits to this site, and stops choosing ads based on your interests. You still
see ads, but they are not personalised.

If your browser sends a [Global Privacy Control](https://globalprivacycontrol.org/)
signal, Google treats it as the same opt-out in the states whose laws require
that.

The information involved is what Google receives when an ad loads: your IP
address, an identifier stored in a cookie, your browser details and the pages
you visit here. It is used to show ads, to measure them and to protect against
fraud.

## Hosting

The site is served by [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com). Like any web
server, Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser details with every
request, and uses them to deliver the pages and to protect the site from
attacks. We do not use Cloudflare's analytics. Cloudflare's own handling of
this information is described in its
[privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## What stays in your browser

Some features save small settings in your browser's local storage. They stay on
your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else:

- your choice of light or dark theme;
- which tab you last picked in a set of tabbed code examples.

Search runs entirely in your browser. What you type in the search box is not
sent anywhere.

The [live demo](/demo/) is a Bellui app running in your browser. Like any Bellui
web app, it can keep its own settings in local storage and its files in your
browser's IndexedDB, and it may register a small service worker to show
notifications. All of this stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing
this site's data in your browser settings.

## Links to other sites

Pages link to GitHub, pkg.go.dev and other sites. When you follow a link, that
site's own privacy policy applies.

## Children

The site is written for software developers and is not aimed at children.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information is
held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to its use. We do
not hold personal information about visitors ourselves. The information
described above is held by Google and Cloudflare, and the links in each section
explain how to make a request to them. You can always write to us and we will
help.

## Changes to this policy

If what the site collects changes, this page changes with it, and the date at
the top shows when it last did. The full history is in the site's
[repository](https://github.com/belluikit/website/commits/main/content/privacy/_index.md).
